Privacy notice

DXF Preflight reads and repairs your DXF files inside your browser. The files are not sent to any server operated by us. We do not use accounts, cookies for tracking, or analytics on this site.

What is stored on your device

The quote calculator saves the rates you type in your browser's local storage, so they are there next time. This data stays on your device. Clear your browser data to remove it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when pages are loaded, under its own privacy policy.

Shop Pack

The paid Shop Pack is sold through Gumroad, which handles payment, taxes and delivery and receives the data needed for that. See Gumroad's own privacy policy. The Shop Pack app also runs offline on your computer and does not upload files.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is. Check repaired files, measurements and prices before cutting or quoting a job.
