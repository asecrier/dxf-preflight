What it checks

Open contours (gaps that make LightBurn/CAM report "open shapes"), duplicate overlapping entities (double cuts), tiny segments, units (DXF files often do not store them; a wrong unit gives a part 25.4× too big or small), and shapes with branching lines. Overlapping partial lines are not detected. This tool is provided as is, without warranty; verify before cutting expensive material.
